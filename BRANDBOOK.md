01  BRAND AT A GLANCE
A cleaner that earns the second bottle.
Ghar Essentials makes kitchen and home cleaners for Indian households, bought online and delivered to the door. Bulk liquid is sourced from a manufacturer in 
Gujarat; bottling, labelling and quality checks are ours.
WHAT WE SELL
Kitchen oil and grease cleaner, lemongrass, 500 ml.
Bathroom, floor and kitchen tools to follow.
WHO BUYS IT
Women aged 24-45 in Indian metros and tier-2 cities,
ordering on a phone, often paying cash on delivery.
WHY THEY SWITCH
Supermarket cleaners are harsh, poorly labelled and
sold by companies you cannot ask a question.
WHAT WE PROMISE
It cuts kitchen grease. You can read the label and
understand it. We answer when you write to us.
The one sentence
“A kitchen cleaner that tells you 
exactly what is in it, and cuts 
grease anyway.”
Transparency is the differentiator, not gentleness. This product is 
alkaline and effective — we say so plainly and tell people how to 
use it sensibly. See slide 11.

The lightness rule
A page background must sit between 95.5% and 97% lightness. Below that it stops reading as a room and starts 
reading as a coloured box — which is exactly what went wrong in v1.

07  TONE OF VOICE
How we write
Plain, specific, unhurried. We explain rather than persuade — the honesty is the sales pitch.
Say this Not this
Cuts baked-on oil from your stove and chimney filter. Revolutionary cleaning technology for a sparkling home!
500 ml. Roughly 40 uses if you dilute as directed. Long-lasting value pack.
Alkaline (pH 10). Keep away from aluminium and marble. Safe on all surfaces.
Written to us? We reply within a working day. 24x7 world-class customer delight.
Hinglish is allowed where it is how people actually speak — “bina harsh chemicals”, “ghar ki safai”. Never forced, never in legal or safety copy, and never more than one Hindi phrase per 
section. English spellings follow Indian usage.

08  PACKAGING & LABEL
Label
Every declaration below is required on a pre-packaged commodity sold in India. None of them are optional.
ghar essentials
Kitchen Cleaner
Oil & grease  ·  Lemongrass
Net quantity: 500 ml
MRP Rs 299 (incl. of all taxes)
Mfd / Packed: 08 / 2026
Packed by: [name & full address]
Consumer care: [phone] · [email]
Country of origin: India
Batch no.: [per batch]
Keep out of reach of children. Avoid contact with eyes. Do not 
mix with other cleaners. Wash hands after use.
Required by Legal Metrology
• Name and complete address of the packer
• Common or generic name of the commodity
• Net quantity in metric units
• Month and year of packing
• Retail sale price as “MRP Rs ___ incl. of all taxes”
• Consumer care name, address, phone and email
• Country of origin
Safety, because the SDS says so
The supplier's own safety sheet classifies this product as an eye 
irritant. That fact decides the label copy, not our marketing 
preference.
• Keep out of reach of children
• Avoid contact with eyes; rinse well if it happens
• Do not mix with other cleaners, especially acidic ones
• Wash hands after use
• Not for aluminium, marble or other natural stone

11  CLAIMS & COMPLIANCE
What we may and may not say
ASCI's Green Claims Guidelines have been in force since February 2024: an environmental or safety claim needs evidence held before publication. We do not yet 
hold it.
BLOCKED UNTIL SUBSTANTIATED
• “Gentle on your hands” — the supplier's SDS classifies the product as an eye irritant
• “Safe around food and kids” — same reason, and it invites unsafe handling
• “Plant-based”, “natural”, “chemical-free” — only 0-3% of the formula is disclosed to us
• “Biodegradable”, “eco-friendly” — the SDS records no biodegradability data at all
• “Safe on all surfaces” — it is alkaline; aluminium and natural stone are excluded
• Any percentage, certification or test result we cannot produce on request
SAFE TO SAY TODAY
• Cuts kitchen oil and grease
• Lemongrass fragrance  ·  500 ml  ·  alkaline, pH 10
• Bottled in Gujarat  ·  country of origin India
• Full ingredient list published on the product page
TO UNBLOCK A CLAIM
• Supplier SDS on their own letterhead, current, with their details filled in
• Written explanation of pH 10 on the COA versus pH 11 on the SDS
• Full ingredient list with percentages, under NDA if needed
• Independent NABL-accredited test for pH and active matter

12  FESTIVE MODE
Festive mode
Diwali 2026 falls on Sunday 8 November; traffic peaks about two weeks before. The palette does not change — only the accent's job does.
WHAT CHANGES
• Accent yellow moves from badges only to the 
announcement bar as well
• One extra band section for the festive bundle
• Gift-note field at checkout
• Deeper gold #E0A020 permitted for festive artwork 
only
What never changes: the page tint, the fonts, the card 
structure, or the claims rules.
DISCOUNT DEPTH
15 - 25%
delivered through bundles, not price cuts on single 
bottles.
A bundle raises the order value and protects the single-bottle 
price you will still be charging in December. A deep cut on one 
bottle teaches people to wait for the next sale.
